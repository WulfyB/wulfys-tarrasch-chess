# Opening book credits

`WolfBook.bin`, the opening book used by Wolf Eval and Wulfy's Tarrasch, is built from the work
of these people and projects. Thank you to all of them.

## The Week in Chess (TWIC), by Mark Crowther

- **Website:** https://theweekinchess.com
- **Editor:** Mark Crowther, who has edited TWIC every week since its first issue on
  17 September 1994.
- **What we use:** the free weekly PGN downloads, issues 920 (August 2012) to the latest. These
  are games from strong over-the-board tournaments around the world.
- **How we use it:** only to count how often positions occur in the first 20 moves of games where
  both players are rated 2200 or more. The book stores a fingerprint (hash) of each position and
  that count. It contains no games, moves, names, events or other TWIC text, and nothing from TWIC
  is redistributed.
- **Support TWIC:** donate via the website. The donation also gets you Mark Crowther's full TWIC
  database. If you use TWIC games in your own project, please credit and support TWIC too.

## Lichess Elite Database, by nikonoel

- **Website:** https://database.nikonoel.fr (Lichess team: https://lichess.org/team/lichess-elite-database)
- **What we use:** monthly collections of lichess.org games between players rated 2400+ and
  2200+, excluding bullet. These are filtered from the Lichess open database (https://database.lichess.org).
- **How we use it:** the same position counting as above. No games are redistributed.
- **Support it:** donate via the website.

## lichess-org/chess-openings, by Lichess

- **Repository:** https://github.com/lichess-org/chess-openings
- **License:** CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication). A copy of the license is in `lichess/COPYING.txt`.
- **What we use:** the 3,865 named opening lines with ECO codes, for opening names and (T)/(O)
  detection. A copy of the files is in `book/lichess`.
- **Support Lichess:** https://lichess.org/patron
